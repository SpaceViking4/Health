## Symptom Patterns

### Daily Pattern
- Morning: worst period — intense depression, sense of emptiness, numbness, hopelessness ("it's too late," "I'm never going to get anywhere")
- Afternoon/evening: lifts significantly, sometimes by half or more; occasionally feels almost absent
- This is clinically known as **diurnal mood variation**, a hallmark of melancholic depression

### Weekly Pattern
- **Weekends**: predominantly depression, more intense (less structure = more rumination)
- **Weekdays**: both depression and anxiety; anxiety likely driven by work/external demands; structure provides some protection against depression
